IDCTE is a volunteer-driven organization sustained entirely by the diaspora community. Monthly gifts give us the stability to plan ahead — sustaining our advocacy in Geneva and Brussels and our work training the next generation of Eelam Tamil activists. One-time gifts are just as valuable and help us respond quickly when urgent situations demand it.
