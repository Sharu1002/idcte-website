## Who We Are

International Diplomatic Council of Tamil Eelam, also known as IDCTE, is a human rights organization registered in Copenhagen, Denmark, since August 2021. IDCTE was founded by a group of new-generation Eelam Tamil youths from Nordic and other European countries who were simply frustrated by the delayed justice for the people of Tamileelam.

## What We Do

IDCTE is a dedicated human rights organization that continuously and tirelessly advocates for the rights, self-determination and justice of the Eelam Tamils in Tamileelam. We firmly believe that every individual deserves to live a life free from oppression and discrimination.

## Our Primary Goal

Eelam Tamils have faced, and continue to face, genocide in Tamileelam by the occupying Sri Lankan state. The Sri Lankan state has carried out genocide against Eelam Tamils since 1948, with the genocide reaching its peak in 2009 — killing tens of thousands of Eelam Tamils in the final days of the armed conflict and leaving at least 146,679 Eelam Tamils unaccounted for. Today we face structural genocide, with the intention of erasing our identity, culture, and literature.

Our primary objective is to raise awareness about the Tamil genocide committed against Eelam Tamils by the Sri Lankan state. We aim to shed light on the mass atrocities and injustices endured by our people, ensuring their stories are heard by the international community. By bringing attention to the Tamil genocide, we seek to create a sense of urgency and drive concrete action for accountability and justice.

Moreover, we advocate for a political solution that addresses the Tamil nation's political aspirations, based on our right to self-determination in Tamileelam.
