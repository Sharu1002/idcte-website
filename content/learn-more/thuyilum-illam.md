title: "Thuyilum Illam: Destroyed Memorial Sites"
teaser: "Satellite evidence of the destruction of Tamil Eelam's Thuyilum Illam, from 1995 to after the war."

---
Thuyilum Illam were the cemeteries across the Tamil Eelam where maaveerar were laid to rest and where families gathered to honour the loved ones they lost in the war.

The destruction of these sites began during the war. When the Sri Lankan army took control of Jaffna in 1995, it bulldozed the cemeteries at Kopay, Kodikamam and Ellankulam. Kopay was rebuilt during the 2002 ceasefire, then destroyed again, and an army camp now stands on it. After the war ended in 2009, every remaining Thuyilum Illam was systematically bulldozed. Families still face restrictions and bans when they try to mourn at these sites.

Using satellite imagery, IDCTE compared 13 sites before and after their destruction: Kanakapuram, Theravil, Vannivelankulam, Kandaladi (Vakarai), Chatty, Ellankulam, Alampil, Aandankulam, Kanchikudicharu, Kopay, Mulankavil, Mulliyawalai and Uduthurai. Use the slider on each site to see what was there and what remains.

This research was first carried out and published by IDCTE in 2021.
